2 · Start screen — animated

Looping preview — on device it runs once, ~1.2s.

0.0s · the ring draws itself The mark assembles, it doesn't just appear

A soft tile rises and the coral→mint ring traces itself clockwise in about a quarter of a second, so the first thing you see is the brand doing something rather than a static image.

0.4s · the bead lands A captured moment settles into the ring

The coral bead springs into the ring's opening — a tiny physical “pop” that hints at capture: a thought, a mood, a photo, a voice note.

0.6s · the wordmark rises Name and promise fade up together

“LifeOS” and “Capture your life” slide up under the mark, then a slim progress shimmer runs while the on-device database opens — no dead spinner, no blank white flash.

1.2s · it hands off Blends into Home, no hard cut

The splash gently scales and fades as Home fades in beneath it, so cold start feels like one continuous motion instead of two screens.

Notes for the build Replaces the plain system splash

The current start is Android's default white splash with the old icon. This keeps the fast native splash covering the encrypted-database open, then plays this animation on top — and respects “reduce motion” by falling straight through to Home. Colours stay in AURA.
